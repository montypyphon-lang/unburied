What's in the game

Everything here is in the build you download today.

Last human standing

Up to 40 survivors per match. Bring friends or let bots fill the plane. The zone closes in five circles until one human, or one squad, is left.

Death turns you

Die and you get back up as a zombie: faster, tougher, able to pounce. Find a cure or take down survivors to become human again.

The dead keep rising

Zombies claw out of graveyards and out of the ground near you all match long. Shamblers, runners and worse.

Deadvale

A 2 × 2 km overgrown valley: forest, swamp, highlands, a river and lakes you can swim, and ruined buildings you can walk into and loot.

Five guns, two slots

MP5, Makarov, double-barrel, AKM and a scoped SV-98, each with its own ammo, recoil and reload. Plus bat, machete and fists.

Loot that matters

Bandages, first aid kits, energy drinks, body armour, backpacks, frag grenades, petrol bombs, and supply drops worth fighting over.

Move like you mean it

Sprint, crouch, dodge roll, vault walls, climb ledges, swim and dive. Barrels and propane tanks explode, and fire spreads.

Play together

Host a party and invite friends, pick squads or free-for-all, or find a game on a server. Lobby chat, ready-up, teammate markers.

Free, and it updates itself

No store, no battle pass, no currency. XP, levels and daily challenges track your career, and the game updates from the lobby.

How a match goes

  1. Ready up

    Gather your party in the lobby. When everyone's ready, the plane leaves.

  2. Drop

    Pick your spot on the map, jump from the ramp, steer the skydive and open your chute.

  3. Loot & survive

    Search chests, gear up, and stay inside the zone while the dead rise around you.

  4. Win, or turn

    Be the last human alive. If you fall, hunt the living and earn your way back.
